Where the defaults come from

Conversion lift (+32%) — Liftoff Creative Index, as reported in independent summaries of playable versus video performance. Competing figures range from +32% to +319%; the lower bound is used here deliberately. Seepia, Hookin

Playable production ($3k–$5k) — AppAgent puts external playable production at $3,000–$5,000 per creative with a two-to-four week turnaround; complex custom builds run higher. AppAgent

Video production — agency video is normally quoted per finished minute, at roughly $3,000–$20,000. A single short ad sits at the low end of that. Industry pricing guides

GCC CPMs — Meta in Saudi Arabia at roughly $8–15 and the UAE $6.5–12; TikTok and Snapchat run below Meta in the same markets. Agency-reported, directional only. BIMO, 23HubLab

Ramadan — Meta CPMs across GCC markets averaged about 68% above non-Ramadan levels in 2026, up from 54% in 2024. Worth modelling separately before a seasonal campaign. BIMO

What this model does not know. Every published benchmark for this format comes from a company that sells the format, and the spread between them is far too wide to treat any single figure as fact. Non-gaming verticals are thinner still — most of the data is from mobile games. So this calculator does not tell you what a playable will do. It tells you what has to be true for one to pay off at your spend, and that is a question a pilot answers, not a deck.
